# Two climatology floors, not one

MAE is compared against the observed percentile's mean absolute deviation
about its across-year median (`obs_mad`). RMSE is compared against its standard
deviation (`obs_sd`).

For a normal sample the first is about 0.8 of the second. Scoring MAE against
the SD therefore understates the error by roughly a fifth, and made DJF appear
to beat a floor that is by construction unreachable.
